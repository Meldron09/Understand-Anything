# Depth-one subagents, a single-writer plan, and bounded concurrency

Subagents are an optimization for independent, bulky work, so the spec uses them at four points only: sources, chunk batches of a large source, lesson units, and (as bounded Bash jobs, not subagents) a video unit's scenes. Lesson units are independent by construction: the router never lets one unit consume another's lesson artifact, so plan order is presentation order and `plan.md` carries no dependency graph. Intake, the router and the comprehension check run inline; ingest runs inline below about 15 PDF pages or 20k tokens, otherwise one subagent per source with about 5 chunk summaries per subagent. Each unit runs in one subagent, except a plan with exactly one unit, which runs inline (the same condition that skips the plan gate).

The conductor spawns subagents with an ad-hoc `Agent` call (no plugin agents), and subagents spawn none. A handoff brief is about 10 lines of paths plus one task sentence, never pasted content. The result is at most about 150 words ending in a footer: `status: done | degraded | failed`, `artifact: <path>`, `reason`, optional `notes`. Anything over about 100 words goes to disk and returns as a path. The conductor is the only writer of `plan.md` and copies `status` from the footer; a subagent writes only its lesson artifact and `./learn-anything/<lesson-slug>/work/<unit-id>/`, which is deleted on `done`, kept on `degraded` or `failed`, and cleared before a re-dispatch.

At most 3 subagents run at once; a video unit is one subagent that writes all scene scripts and renders them with `min(2, cores/4)` parallel Bash jobs, with renderer-internal workers at 1. These numbers are untested defaults to retune after a Mac spike. The renderer fallback and silent-video rungs of ADR 0001 happen inside the video subagent; on `failed` or a missing footer the conductor re-dispatches the unit once, straight to text, recorded as `degraded: <reason>`, after converting the unit's size to words (original size times the router's rate for the original mode gives minutes, times 200 words per minute: `2 min` of video becomes 400 words, `12 slides` 3,600, `10 quiz items` 1,000), so the learner's time budget is preserved; a re-dispatch that also fails is `failed: <reason>` and reported. Cost is shown, not metered: the plan gate prints units, subagents, video minutes, tier and download size, the router caps a plan at 8 units and 5 minutes of video (an over-cap plan goes back to the learner to cut, never silently shrunk), and there is no token budget. Chunk summaries use the `haiku` alias; everything else inherits the session model; no model ids are hard-coded.

Rejected: one subagent per scene or per chunk (a brief and skill load per call to run a shell command or a one-line summary), nested subagents (unbounded fan-out and cost, harder to reason about failure), subagents writing `plan.md` (parallel units would race on one file), plugin-defined agents (unverified `context: fork` and plugin-scoped agent behaviour, ticket 14), and a dependency graph between units (dependent units, such as a quiz built from the explainer's emphasis, wait for v2).
